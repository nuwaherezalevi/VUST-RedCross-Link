MEMBERSHIP PROCESS

How membership works.

The Constitution describes the following admission steps. The Link can confirm current forms, fees where applicable and registration arrangements.

  1. Submit an applicationProvide the information requested through the official membership process.
  2. Executive Committee reviewApplications are subject to approval by the Executive Committee.
  3. Complete registrationComplete registration and any prescribed fees where applicable.
  4. Undertake the Volunteer OathCommit to the Constitution and the Fundamental Principles.

LEARN & GROW

Build skills for meaningful service.

The Constitution identifies potential training areas including First Aid, Basic Life Support, Disaster Risk Reduction, Community Health, WASH, Psychological First Aid, leadership, volunteer management, climate adaptation, environmental conservation, safeguarding, project management, monitoring and evaluation, proposal writing and digital literacy.

Training availability, eligibility and certification depend on the relevant programme and approved training provider.
