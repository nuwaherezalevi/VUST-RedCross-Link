WHO WE ARE

Compassion in action, led by young people.

VUST RedCross Link is a humanitarian student organization established to mobilize students, staff and volunteers in promoting humanitarian values, community resilience, public health, disaster preparedness and volunteer service.

Rooted in service and respect for human dignity, we connect learning with practical action for the wellbeing of our communities.

QUESTIONS?

Good to know before you join.

Find answers to common questions about participation and our work.

Membership information →
Who can become a member?

Membership is open to eligible persons who support the Link's mission and principles, agree to abide by its Constitution and meet applicable University and Uganda Red Cross Society requirements.

Do I need previous humanitarian experience?

Contact the Link to learn about current opportunities and any training or role requirements. Continuous learning and capacity building are part of the Link's work.

Can community members participate?

The Link is community-oriented and undertakes community outreach. Contact us to ask about current public activities and participation arrangements.

How can an organization partner with us?

Send a partnership enquiry outlining your organization, proposed area of collaboration and contact person. Partnerships are subject to the Link's approval and principles.

Can I request First Aid or emergency assistance here?

This website is not an emergency-response service. For urgent emergencies, contact the appropriate local emergency services. Enquiries here do not guarantee deployment or service.
