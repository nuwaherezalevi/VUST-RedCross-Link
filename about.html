ABOUT THE LINK

Who we are, and what guides us.

A student-led humanitarian community working to turn compassion into responsible action.

OUR BACKGROUND

Learning, leadership and service in one community.

VUST RedCross Link is established to mobilize students, staff and volunteers in promoting humanitarian values, community resilience, public health, disaster preparedness and volunteer service. It provides a framework for members to participate in humanitarian programmes, develop their capabilities and contribute to sustainable community development.

Its Constitution sets out the Link's governance, membership, leadership, financial management, volunteer engagement, partnerships and programme implementation. The Constitution identifies the Link as voluntary, humanitarian, non-political, non-sectarian, non-profit-making, student-led and community-oriented.

OUR VISION

A generation that transforms lives.

To be the leading humanitarian student organization at Valley University of Science and Technology, inspiring compassionate, resilient and socially responsible leaders who transform lives and strengthen communities.

OUR MISSION

Mobilizing people for humanity.

To mobilize and empower the VUST community to prevent and alleviate human suffering through humanitarian service, health promotion, disaster preparedness and response, community resilience, and the promotion of human dignity in accordance with the Fundamental Principles of the International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement.

THE SEVEN FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLES

Principles that guide every action.

01

Humanity

Prevent and alleviate human suffering; protect life, health and dignity.

02

Impartiality

Assist without discrimination and prioritize those most in need.

03

Neutrality

Do not take sides in political, racial, religious or ideological controversies.

04

Independence

Maintain humanitarian autonomy while cooperating with public authorities.

05

Voluntary Service

Serve voluntarily and not for financial gain.

06

Unity

Recognize the Uganda Red Cross Society as Uganda's single National Society.

07

Universality

Share responsibilities within the worldwide Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement.

GOVERNANCE

Accountability is part of service.

The Constitution describes a governance framework comprising the Patron, General Assembly, Executive Committee, Standing Committees, Departmental Working Groups, registered members and volunteers. The General Assembly is the supreme decision-making organ, while the Executive Committee manages day-to-day affairs.
